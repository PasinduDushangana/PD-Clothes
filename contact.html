Before you order

How do I place an order?

Choose a bag and select “Buy.” Add your contact and delivery details, then send the prepared request on WhatsApp. We’ll confirm availability, delivery, and payment with you.

How do I pay?

We’ll confirm payment arrangements in WhatsApp. This website does not collect online payments.

What does delivery cost?

Share your delivery location in chat. We’ll confirm available options, costs, and timing before you place your order.

Can I ask about a sold-out bag?

Yes. Open its product page and use “Ask about availability” to message us about that specific bag.

What if I need help after ordering?

Message us with your order details so we can help. Please confirm any return or exchange terms before placing your order.
